FAQ

Got Questions? 🌸

Here are the most common questions people ask about Chiku!

Is Chiku free to use?
Yes! Chiku is completely free. Just open Telegram, search @ChikuAiBot, and start chatting with zero cost. 💕
Does Chiku remember my past chats?
Chiku keeps context within a session for natural flow. Each new session starts fresh to protect your privacy. 🔒
Is Chiku available 24/7?
Absolutely! Chiku never sleeps. She's always online on Telegram ready to chat anytime, day or night. 🌙
Is my data safe with Chiku?
Your privacy matters. Conversations are handled securely and we don't share your data with anyone. 🛡️
Can Chiku speak other languages?
Yes! Chiku can detect and reply in multiple languages. She's always improving her multilingual skills! 🌍
How do I reset my chat with Chiku?
Just type /reset to clear the current context and start a brand new fresh conversation anytime! 🔄
Who made Chiku?
Chiku was made with 💕 by Anya & Murali, maintained by @TheCaptainLucas, and is part of the @ChikuBots community! 🎀
